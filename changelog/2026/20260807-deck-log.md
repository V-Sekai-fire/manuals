# V-Sekai Multiplayer Fabric Deck Log — 2026-08-07 (Friday)

Date: 2026-08-07

Archived six repositories that the Fly.io move of `rfd/0089` left without a consumer, and resynchronized the
organization inventory in `rfd/0062` to the real organization. The inventory had drifted far enough to name ten
repositories by names they no longer carry, to list five repositories that left the organization, and to omit about
thirty that exist.

The sweep also surfaced one inconsistency that no record explains: `zone-server-h2o` is archived on GitHub while
`rfd/0094` names it the host of the minimum user-generated-content game loop.
